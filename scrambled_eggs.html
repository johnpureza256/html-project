Scrambled Eggs

All Recipes

Description

Heavy cream and Cheddar cheese make fluffy, soft, rich scrambled eggs with great flavor.

Allrecipes home cooks applaud the recipe for being easy for beginners and kids, too. "My family absolutely went nuts, and I had to make more." —Allrecipes member NoblePea9072

Ingredients:

  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 thin slices cooked ham, diced (Optional)
  • 2 tablespoons sliced Cheddar cheese
  • 1 teaspoon heavy whipping cream
  • ½ teaspoon butter

Steps:

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Whisk eggs in a small bowl until smooth. Mix in ham, Cheddar cheese, and heavy cream.
  3. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat.
  4. Pour in egg mixture; cook and stir until set but still moist, 3 to 5 minutes.
